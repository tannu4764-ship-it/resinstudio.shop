About My Resin Studio

My Resin Studio is a dedicated handmade art studio based in Govind Nagar, Kanpur. We specialise in creating unique handmade resin products, personalised resin gifts, custom resin artwork, and aesthetic resin home décor for homes, special celebrations, and meaningful gifting.

When searching for resin art in Govind Nagar or seeking custom resin artwork Kanpur residents can cherish, people often look for pieces that feel thoughtful and personal rather than mass-manufactured. Our studio was created to give local customers direct access to handmade craftsmanship, allowing you to choose your own colour themes, incorporate meaningful names or dates, and preserve sentimental items into long-lasting artwork.

From bespoke entrance nameplates and resin wall clocks to delicate botanical keychains and wedding floral keepsakes, every piece is individually poured, cured, and finished right here in our Kanpur studio.

How We Work

The Custom-Order Process

Ordering bespoke resin art is simple and transparent from start to finish.

1

Consultation & Idea Sharing

Send us your product idea, required dimensions, colour choices, and any text or photos via WhatsApp or our enquiry form.

2

Design Confirmation & Quote

We discuss colour swatches, lettering styles, and confirm the exact pricing in rupees (₹) along with the curing timeline.

3

Layered Pouring & Curing

Your piece is carefully hand-poured in layers, allowing proper time between pours for resin curing and bubble elimination.

4

Finishing & Handover

Edges are hand-sanded smooth, inspected for quality, and prepared for studio pickup in Govind Nagar or doorstep delivery.
