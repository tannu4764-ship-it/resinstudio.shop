Cherish Milestones Forever

Wedding Varmala & Floral Memory Preservation in Resin

Do not let your precious wedding garlands dry and fade away. At My Resin Studio in Govind Nagar, Kanpur, we professionally preserve wedding varmala rose petals, milestone photos, and sacred ceremony keepsakes inside crystal-clear, heirloom-grade epoxy resin blocks.

🌸 How Our Floral Preservation Process Works

Step 1

Flower Handover

Deliver your wedding garlands (varmala) to our Govind Nagar studio within 24 to 48 hours of your event for optimum floral freshness.

Step 2

Silica Dehydration

Flowers undergo gentle multi-day silica gel dehydration to preserve the vibrant natural red, pink, and white pigments without browning.

Step 3

Multi-Layer Casting

Cast in bubble-free, UV-resistant resin with 24k gold leaf flakes, custom photos, or marriage dates, followed by diamond edge polishing.
