# AGENTS.md — My Resin Studio

This document defines the project guidelines, technical rules, audience, and working principles for **My Resin Studio**. All future tasks and agents in this workspace must strictly follow the rules outlined below.

---

## 1. Project Overview

My Resin Studio is a handcrafted resin art business based in Govind Nagar, Kanpur, creating unique handmade resin products, personalised gifts, custom resin artwork and aesthetic home décor. The studio focuses on creative, visually appealing and personalised resin products made for gifting, special occasions and personal use.

---

## 2. Audience

Men and women aged 18 to 45 living within 5 kilometres of Govind Nagar, Kanpur. They are looking for unique, personalised and aesthetically pleasing gifts or home décor and may compare different local handmade businesses based on product quality, creativity, customisation, pricing, delivery time and trustworthiness.

---

## 3. Primary Goal

Every page must push the visitor toward placing a custom-order enquiry or contacting the studio through WhatsApp.

---

## 4. Tech Rules

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript only.
- No frameworks.
- No build step.
- No external UI libraries.
- One shared `style.css` and one shared `script.js`.
- Mobile-first CSS.
- Semantic HTML5 tags.

---

## 5. Working Rules

- Build one page at a time.
- Never create files I did not ask for.
- Explain what you changed after each task.
- Ask me any question you need answered before writing the file.
